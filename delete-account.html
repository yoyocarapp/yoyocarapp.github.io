Delete Your YoYoCar Account

Last updated: 26 May 2026 · Operated by the YoYoCar team

This page explains how to permanently delete your YoYoCar account and the personal data we hold about you. Deletion is processed immediately in the app; in rare cases we may retain limited data for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention reasons (see below).

How to delete your account

  1. Open the YoYoCar app on your phone and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Account tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll to the Support section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the warning, then tap Delete forever to confirm.
  6. You will be signed out immediately. Your account is now deleted.

What is deleted

The following personal data is permanently and irrevocably removed:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth
  • Your profile photo and bio
  • Your authentication tokens and device push-notification tokens
  • The ability to sign back into the account

What may be retained

For safety, legal, and operational reasons we may keep the following in an anonymized form (with no link back to your real identity):

If you can't access the app

If you have lost access to your phone or the app, email support@yoyocar.com from the email address linked to your YoYoCar account, with the subject line Delete my YoYoCar account. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

More information

Full details of what data we collect, how we use it, and how to exercise your other privacy rights are in our Privacy Policy.
